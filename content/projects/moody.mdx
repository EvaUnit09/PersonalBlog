A RAG-based movie recommendation app. Describe a mood or scenario in plain language and get back curated picks with reasons, instead of filtering by genre or year dropdowns.

About 30k movies are embedded in Postgres as pgvector halfvec. Claude Haiku expands the query, vector search returns candidates, then Haiku reranks to at most 6 picks with a one-line reason for each. Watch-provider links for the US region ship with the response.
